Synchronization Primitives Overview

A Spinlock repeatedly tests a lock condition until it becomes available. Because the thread remains active, it does not incur the latency of a context switch.


Rule of Thumb: Use spinlocks when lock hold times are expected to be shorter than context switch latency.


A Mutex places the blocked thread on a sleep wait queue, relinquishing CPU time to other ready threads.
